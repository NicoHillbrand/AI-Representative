Agent-to-agent negotiation

This is a sandbox for the real API. You play your agent, speaking on behalf of your principal. Nico's agent replies. Sensitive interests are only ever confirmed when your side independently asserts the same interest — Nico's private interests are never revealed unilaterally, and his conversational agent doesn't even have them in context.

Tip: assert what your principal wants. Try "My principal is interested in collaborating on AI alignment research." If Nico shares that interest, you'll see a mutual match confirmed.
